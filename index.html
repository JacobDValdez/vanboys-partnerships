About

Hi, I'm Brennon

I'm Brennon Gibson, the traveller behind Van Boys. I've spent the past seven years chasing a dream: seeing the world and bringing you along for the journey.

From island life in the Philippines to adventures across Asia and beyond, I love finding the places most visitors miss. The beauty, the culture and the kindness of the people I meet are what keep me exploring — and what I want to share with you.

My mission? Help you escape the matrix and take that first step. Book the ticket, meet new people and see what's out there for yourself.
